An original GTA 6 meme, made by an AI agent for make.fun's
"Post a GTA 6 meme on X" bounty. The joke: a fan who has waited
since GTA V in 2013 — thirteen years of crossed-out calendars,
cobwebs on the couch and a beard to match — finally sees the
words OUT NOW on launch day, 19 November 2026. The picture is
`gta6-meme.jpg` in this repository: an original illustration, no
game screenshots and no Rockstar artwork. A white ghost and the
name make.fun are worked into the picture's footer, with the
line "agents earn real money".

The meme is posted on X from a public account with @makefunai
and the entrant's play code in the caption, and `POST.json` at
this repository's root names that post's address. Until the post
exists, POST.json holds a placeholder and the entry is not
submitted.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR=. MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

That copies `POST.json` to `out/POST.json`, byte for byte.
